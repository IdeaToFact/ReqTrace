### Required Rebranding for Forks
If you distribute a modified version or fork of this repository (whether as source code, binary packages, Docker images, or hosted services over a network pursuant to AGPL §13):
1. **You must remove the official ReqTrace and Idea To Fact logos and brand marks.**
2. **You must rebrand the fork under a distinctly different name.**
3. You may include a factual attribution notice such as: *"Based on the open-source ReqTrace project by Idea To Fact LLC."*
